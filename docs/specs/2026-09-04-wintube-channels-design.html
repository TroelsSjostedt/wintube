Design spec · Stage 6 · Approved 2026-09-04

WinTube Channels

Channels and subscriptions, ported whole from tvOS: the subscriptions feed woven into Home the way the Apple TV composes it, clickable channel pages with a subscribe toggle, and a Subscriptions screen listing every channel the account follows.

Why now The composite Home is where the reliable Shorts row lives — YouTube's bare Home response often carries none Base WinTube stages 1–5

1 · The composite Home (from HomeView.swift)

tvOS Home is a collage of three browse feeds, fetched in parallel and interleaved by a fixed rule. WinTube adopts it verbatim — this is what makes the two devices show the same screen:

RecommendedHome (`default`) — shelves up to and including the first non-Shorts row
From your subscriptionsFEsubscriptions — first row, retitled
ShortsFEsubscriptions — its remaining rows, Shorts row included
New to you, …Home — the rest; "Load more" grows this part
Continue watchingFEhistory rows, always last
  • FeedService.LoadCompositeHomeAsync fetches default, FEsubscriptions and FEhistory in parallel and merges as above. The lead/trailing split of the Home shelves is positional: everything up to and including the first non-Shorts section leads (a response opening with a Shorts row keeps it in the lead).
  • Supplementary failure is silent. A failed subscriptions or history fetch just leaves its rows out — Home renders regardless. Only the Home fetch itself can fail the page.
  • The subscriptions feed folds like History: untitled chunk rows fold into the titled row above (existing FoldUntitledRows), the first non-Shorts row is retitled "From your subscriptions", its Shorts row passes through untouched.
  • Paging: the page-level continuation is Home's own and appends to the trailing Home part, before the history rows. Row (sideways) paging is unchanged. Each response's own Shorts handling (Merging) is per-feed; the composite adds one cross-feed pass that drops from later Shorts rows any id an earlier one already shows — a Short in the subscriptions row is not repeated by a Home or history one.

2 · Core services

PieceDecision
FeedService.LoadChannelAsync(id)A channel is just another browseId (UC…). Same browse call, same shelf parsing; new header parse for title, avatar, banner (16:9, may be absent) and IsSubscribed read from the page's own subscribe button — null when the page carried no button, which callers must treat as "unknown", not "no".
ChannelPage model(Title, AvatarUrl, BannerUrl, IsSubscribed, Feed) — Feed is an ordinary FeedPage, so the channel screen reuses every row behavior Home has, Shorts rows included.
SubscriptionServiceSubscribeAsync/UnsubscribeAsync post subscription/subscribe|unsubscribe with channelIds: [id]; success is any 2xx. LoadSubscriptionsAsync browses FEchannels and returns the channels in response order plus the id set.
FEchannels fallbackPorted from tvOS: if the cells don't parse but UC… ids are found, list those bare (avatar-less tiles that still open the channel) rather than showing an empty screen. Ids are collected in deterministic order.
VideoItem.ChannelIdNew nullable field: the first UC… browse id in the cell, as the tvOS parser reads it. Feeds the click-through and the context menu.
Not portedtvOS's on-disk subscription cache (a TV-startup optimization) and its ChannelAvatarService — the FEchannels cells carry the avatars the grid needs.

3 · The channel page

AspectDecision
HeaderBanner (when present) drawn behind; avatar 64 px circle, channel name, and the subscribe toggle on one line beneath it.
Subscribe toggleOne button: "Subscribe" (outline) ↔ "Subscribed ✓" (filled). Toggles optimistically and rolls back with the error surfaced in the page's InfoBar if the call fails. Hidden while IsSubscribed is unknown.
RowsThe channel's shelves rendered exactly as Home rows: VideoCard rows, ShortCard rows, sideways paging. First page of shelves only — no "Load more" (the tvOS scope choice).
NavigationFrame.Navigate(ChannelPage, channelId + fallback title). The fallback title from the originating card shows until the header arrives. Channel pages can open further channel pages; system back walks the stack.
Empty/errorLoad failure: InfoBar with Retry, as every other page. A channel with no shelves says so in one quiet line.

4 · The Subscriptions screen

  • New Subscriptions item in the NavigationView, between Search and History.
  • A wrapping grid of the followed channels: 96 px round avatar with the name beneath, in the order FEchannels returned them (YouTube's own order — no local sorting). Click opens the channel page.
  • Loaded on first visit, refreshed on every navigation to the page; no disk cache.

5 · Ways into a channel

EntryBehavior
Channel name on a cardThe subtitle's channel-name segment becomes a link (hover underline). Click opens the channel instead of the video.
Right-click a cardContext menu with Go to channel — the mouse idiom for tvOS's long-press menu. Shown only when the cell carried a ChannelId.
Player title barThe channel name shown with the playing video becomes the same link.
Subscriptions gridEvery tile, as above.

6 · Testing

  • Core tests against synthetic JSON, as all stages: composite merge order (lead / subscriptions / trailing / history), silent supplementary failure, subscriptions-feed folding + retitle, channel header parse (present and absent banner/button), FEchannels parse with the bare-id fallback, subscribe/unsubscribe request shape, ChannelId extraction.
  • UI verified by Claude via app launch + screenshots (composite Home order, channel page, grid); subscribe/unsubscribe exercised against a real channel and confirmed on another device.

7 · Deliberately out

  • Channel search, channel tabs (Videos/Playlists/About) — the TV response's shelves are the page.
  • Notifications/bell state — not in tvOS either.
  • Disk-cached subscription list and avatar lookup service — fetch on demand.
