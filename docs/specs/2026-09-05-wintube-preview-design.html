Design spec · Stage 7 · Approved 2026-09-05

WinTube Preview

A card the pointer rests on — or keyboard focus lands on — plays its video silently in place of the thumbnail, exactly as a focused tvOS card does. Nothing is recorded, nothing is remembered; leaving the card puts the thumbnail back.

tvOS arbiter YouTubeTV/Sources/UI/VideoPreview.swift Base WinTube stages 1–6

1 · Trigger and lifetime

  • Warm = pointer enters the card, or the card's list item takes keyboard focus (Tab / arrow keys). Cold = pointer leaves / focus moves on.
  • A shared PreviewCoordinator owns at most ONE active preview. 0.7 s after warm it starts the stream resolution; cold before then just cancels the timer; cold mid-resolution cancels via the CancellationToken; cold during playback tears down. A new card going warm preempts the old preview immediately.
  • The coordinator's decision logic lives in Core, UI-free, with injected time (PreviewGate: warm/cold/preempt in, "start resolving id X" / "stop" out) so the delay, cancellation and one-at-a-time rules are unit-testable.

2 · The surface (from VideoPreview.swift)

AspectDecision
ElementA second MediaPlayerElement in the card, no transport controls, stretched like the thumbnail (UniformToFill semantics — same box, so the cross-fade doesn't shift the image).
RevealOpacity 0 until the first frame is ready, then a 0.2 s ease-in fade over the thumbnail. The thumbnail never flashes black while resolving.
Sound & stateMuted always. No watch progress written, no history recorded, no SponsorBlock, no screen-keep-awake. Starts from the top at normal speed; opening the video afterwards still resumes from real progress.
VolumeUnaffected by and not affecting the remembered player volume.
FailureSilent: a resolution or playback failure leaves the thumbnail standing. No error UI, no retry ladder beyond what StreamService.ResolveAsync itself does.

3 · Cost control

  • Each preview is a full StreamService.ResolveAsync — the 0.7 s dwell means a pointer sweeping a row costs nothing.
  • One at a time, preemption cancels the loser's resolution; a stale resolution completing after preemption is discarded (token checked before play).

4 · Scope

  • Both card types: VideoCard (landscape, over the thumbnail area only — the caption stays) and ShortCard (the whole portrait tile).
  • Every page that shows cards: Home, Search, History, channel pages, and the Shorts rows on all of them. The Subscriptions grid (channel avatars) has no videos and is untouched.
  • The real player is untouched; a preview never survives navigation (page teardown = cold).

5 · Testing

  • Core: PreviewGate tests with injected time — dwell not yet elapsed, cold cancels timer, preemption stops the previous id, stale completion discarded, cold during playback stops.
  • UI: verified by Claude via app launch + cursor placement + screenshots (fade-in on dwell, thumbnail restored on leave, keyboard focus trigger via Tab/arrows).

6 · Deliberately out

  • Sound on preview, scrubbing, duration cap — tvOS has none of these.
  • Prefetching neighbours' streams.
